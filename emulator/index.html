Drag the aircraft on the tape (or use the slider that appears on the tape) to climb and descend. Callouts arm only after you climb through the dashed ARM line, then fire on the way down. Or hit Fly approach to auto-fly a real 85 kt, 3° ILS descent and flare — sit back and listen to the cadence.

Config menu

The same options the box exposes when you hold the button at power-on. Commit to apply and recalibrate to ground (0 ft).

Audio mode

Start-altitude cap —

Highest callout that will sound. Numbers above this are suppressed on the way down (the presence tone is unaffected).

Tone volume —

Trim on the presence tone only — cut or boost (−6 dB to +6 dB). Drag to audition each level.

Voice volume —

Trim on the spoken callouts only — cut only (0 dB to −6 dB). Drag to audition each level.

Check gear altitude —

Speaks “… check gear” after the number at this height. Off by default.

Config button

The real hold-at-boot menu, on one button. Tap to cycle the current option; double-tap to confirm it and move on. Same prompts, same preview tones, same order the box walks — listen as you go.
